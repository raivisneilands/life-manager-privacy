Account & Data Deletion

Effective date: 04.07.2026

You can delete your Life Manager account and data immediately from within the app, or request deletion here if you no longer have the app installed.

How to Delete Your Account

If you have the app installed: go to Account > Actions and choose to delete your account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all associated data.

If you no longer have the app installed: email lifemanager.tracker@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my account." We'll verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your login credentials
  • Tasks, habits, health entries, finance entries, journal entries, fitness workouts, and goals with their plans
  • Relationships data (name, birthdate, how you met, and relationship type)

This action cannot be undone.

Requesting Deletion of Specific Data

You don't need to delete your whole account to remove specific data:

  • Individual entries — tasks, habits, health entries, finance entries, journal entries, fitness workouts, goals, and relationships can each be deleted directly within the app, immediately and without contacting us.
  • All cloud-stored data, account kept: if you have the app installed, go to Settings > Data to delete your stored data while keeping your account and login active — this takes effect immediately. If you no longer have the app installed, email us at the address above with the subject line "Delete my data" and we'll complete this within 30 days.

What We Retain

We don't retain your data after a deletion request is completed, except where we're required to by law. Purchase records for the app itself are handled by Google Play and aren't something we can delete on your behalf — see Google Play's own policies for that.
